# Problem Statement

## Background

[Modern defense operations rely on continuous telemetry streaming across disparate domains—including enterprise SIEM logs, satellite orbital/RF feeds, cyber sensors, and strategic intelligence reports.
As hybrid warfare escalates, the sheer volume of multi-source incoming signals has exploded, creating an intensely congested and complex operational landscape.]

## The Problem

[Defense analysts receive thousands of unstructured alerts daily in conflicting formats, making manual inspection by human teams virtually impossible.
Critical, multi-stage adversary attacks remain hidden beneath overwhelming noise, forcing operators to guess which signals represent genuine threats versus benign anomalies.]

## Who is Affected

[Frontline defense intelligence analysts and military SOC teams who face severe cognitive exhaustion and alert fatigue while triaging massive data backlogs.
Joint task force commanders and tactical leaders who are starved of timely, actionable situational awareness during high-stakes operational crises.]

## Why It Matters

[Overlooking a single genuine adversary threat can result in catastrophic operational failure, compromised defense infrastructure, and national security breaches.
At the same time, squandering vital personnel and mission-critical resources on investigating false positives paralyzes defense readiness and delays urgent countermeasures.]

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

[Legacy SIEM and security tools operate in isolated silos, failing to correlate ground-based cyber telemetry with satellite, RF, and strategic intelligence feeds.
Furthermore, they produce bloated, raw technical dumps rather than mapping threats to the MITRE ATT&CK framework or delivering structured, decision-ready BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) briefings for commanders.]
